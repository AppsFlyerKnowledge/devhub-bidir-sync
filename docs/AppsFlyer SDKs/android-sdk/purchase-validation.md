AppsFlyer provides two solutions that validate and measure in-app purchases and subscription revenue.

- **ROI360 Stores Revenue** is AppsFlyer’s premium offering for full in-app purchases and subscription measurement. It provides a complete, deduplicated, and enriched revenue data layer that includes refunds, subscription renewals, upgrades, downgrades, and net revenue calculations. [KB link]
- **Receipt Validation** verifies iOS and Android in-app purchases to filter out fraudulent transactions. It’s a free solution that performs server-side validation using AppsFlyer’s updated `validateAndLog` 2.0 API. [KB link]
